# Action plan, 2026-10-28: first contact on a chosen dot

Builds on `docs/rca_touch_dot.md`; revised after `docs/critique_2026-10-28.md` and
`docs/critique_rca_plan_2026-10-28.md`. This is the current plan; `docs/needle_on_dot.md` and
`docs/plan.md` are background. New hardware today: a second camera and a ToF sensor; neither is on the
critical path (step 5).

## The goal and how it is judged

**Gate:** the needle's **first** contact lands **within 2 mm** of the chosen black dot's centre, twice in a
row, then 4 of 5 dots. 2 mm is half a pan pulse (`model_jab.SIDE_TOL_MM`); **1 mm** becomes the gate only
if step 2 shows a fine axis (wrist roll) that moves the tip <= 0.3 mm per pulse at the descent pose.

**Judged by a camera-independent mark:** the tip is inked before each run, so the first contact leaves a
dot of ink; a drag leaves a streak. After the run the UGREEN (SAM 3.1 on the sheet) measures mark centre vs
target dot. The arm stops on a contact signal, but the signal is not the judge.

(A conductive foil under the sheet, RCA 9.1, cannot close a circuit through the paper; the inked tip
replaces it as the independent ground truth.)

**Rule 5 is scaffolding:** placing the target within 20 mm of the needle's natural reach tests the
descent, not reach; colonies on a plate cannot be moved to the arm. A pass is reported with that label.

## Rules for the day

1. **The final approach is watched:** the UGREEN streams during every move below 10 mm; the arm stops on
   the first contact signal. Re-kill `sensor_supervisor_node` after **every** stack restart.
2. **No lateral motion below 8 mm.** All sideways/along error is closed at >= 8 mm; below that the tip moves
   only along one fixed arc with no lateral command, so an early touchdown cannot drag.
3. **Safety: two sources must agree.** Stop when the measured height and the model height differ by more
   than 3 mm, or either reads below the floor. (A pose fit once put the tip 96 mm under the paper; the model
   scale was once 5x off.) `--press 0.0`: no planned motion ends inside the paper.
4. **SAM 3.1 anchors the targets** (dots, cube, the ink mark). The moving tip is tracked by motion
   difference between SAM re-anchors (SAM gives 0 masks on the needle, ~0.3-0.9 s per call, file-in).
   Loop rate is set by the camera (~5-10 Hz), not by SAM.
5. **Offline first, one change per live run, 5-minute time box per attempt;** a failure is logged and
   fixed offline.
6. **Strafe only where pan cannot align;** sideways corrections finish from one direction (backlash).
7. **Tool integrity before each gate run:** needle clamped, re-inked, `lam` re-triangulated from both
   cameras.

## Step 1: servo 2 under load (30 min)

Paths are in `D:/robot/highwonder_rework/`: `hwr_core/hwr_core/rrc/board.py` (`servo_temp`, `servo_vin`),
`tools/live_phase1_bench.py`.

1. Driver stopped: read temperature, voltage, angle limits of every servo.
2. Stack restarted, supervisor re-killed. **Rise test at reach, loaded:** the arm at the descent pose of
   yesterday's targets, servo 2 commanded +20 in 5-pulse steps and back, 3 repeats.
   **Pass:** read-back within 3 pulses of command each step, all repeats.
3. Same at the start pose (423 -> 443) for comparison.

**If it fails at reach:** every motion keeps servo 2 fixed or moving down only (current code already does),
and targets stay near the natural reach. The day goes on.

## Step 2: fine-axis spike and error budget (30 min, in the air)

At the descent pose, angled needle: roll (servo 5) +-1, +-2, +-5 pulses from both directions; elbow and
wrist 1 pulse each. UGREEN measures tip motion per pulse, deadband, backlash. Fill the budget table.
**Decides the gate:** roll <= 0.3 mm/pulse and repeatable -> gate 1 mm; else 2 mm.

## Step 3: contact signal (30 min, offline + a few live descents)

Ground truth: the ink mark (where) and the UGREEN frame stream (when). Five slow automatic wrist-arc
descents (the real motion, not teleop) onto blank paper, frames logged at camera rate. Candidates:

| Signal | From | Note |
|---|---|---|
| tip stops in the image while the command continues | UGREEN motion track | primary |
| wrist/elbow read-back lag grows | servos | backup, debounced; noisy on reversals |
| ToF stops decreasing | ToF | corroboration only; check its minimum range first (VL53L-class is unreliable < ~10 mm, the cone may see the needle) |

Pick the earliest signal that fires on all five with no false fire in the air.

## Step 4: the watched descent = three changes to the live wrist arc (45 min)

`tools/model_jab.py`, final-contact loop (wrist arc after placing):

1. `WRIST_STEP` 3 -> 1 pulse; no sleep-and-grid-pose per step: send, read UGREEN frames continuously.
2. Stop on the step-3 contact signal instead of the arm-camera model height `h <= 0.3` (kept as a backstop
   with rule 3's two-source check).
3. The tip is measured in the UGREEN (tip and dot both visible), not in the arm camera (dot hidden).

Placing stays as is (URDF move, SAM tie, measured corrections) and must close sideways/along error at
>= 8 mm (rule 2), roll used for the last sideways mm if step 2 qualifies it.

Offline check before live: frame logs of step 3 replayed through the new stop logic (record frames to
disk now; a full robot-I/O stub of `model_jab` is a refactor and not today).

## Step 5: side camera and ToF (contingency, 60 min)

Only if the UGREEN cannot see the tip and the dot together at the target (arm occlusion) or step 3 finds no
reliable contact signal. Side camera: needs a standoff it can focus at (or a macro lens), its own
intrinsics, SAM tie on the dots. ToF: corroboration, not an interlock.
