# Action plan, 2026-10-28: first contact on a chosen dot

Builds on `docs/rca_touch_dot.md`. New hardware today: **a second camera** and **a ToF distance sensor**.
Assumed below: the camera is a USB camera like the UGREEN, the ToF is a small single-zone sensor
(VL53L-class: mm output, a few mm noise, ~25 deg cone). Adjust step 2 if they differ.

## The goal and how it is judged

**Gate:** the needle's **first** contact with the paper lands on the chosen black dot, within 1 mm of
its centre, **twice in a row**, then 4 of 5 dots.

**Judged by:** the position of the tip at the frame where contact starts, from a camera watching the
descent (the new side camera and the UGREEN, read continuously). Not the end pose, not a photo after
the move. Every live run writes one row to the run table (section 6).

## Rules for the day (from the RCA)

1. **The final approach is always watched.** The side camera and the UGREEN stream frames during every
   move below 15 mm; the arm stops on first contact.
2. **Safety checks use measurements only** (camera height, ToF, contact signal), never the model, and
   they run in every phase before any new motion type goes live.
3. **Offline first.** Each new version runs on recorded frames (replay harness, step 4) before the arm
   moves. One change per live run.
4. **SAM 3.1 for every detection** (dots, cube, needle); no hand-made thresholds as the detector.
5. **Targets near the natural reach:** the chosen dot within 20 mm of where the needle points at the
   start pose. No full-stretch reaches, no shoulder rises under load.
6. **Strafe only where pan cannot align**, one measured pulse at a time, <= 0.5 s per pulse.
7. **Time box:** a live attempt that has not touched within 5 minutes is stopped, written into the run
   table, and fixed offline.

## Step 0: start-up (15 min)

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Stack up, sensor supervisor stopped | no `sensor_supervisor_node`; arm frame in < 0.3 s |
| Battery | >= 11.8 V on `/battery` |
| Servo 2 rises | commanded 423 -> 443 reads within 3 pulses; down and back |
| Needle rigid | clamped/collared in the gripper; does not move when pushed by hand |
| Notebook and robot | robot base on floor marks, notebook taped, red cube in place, target dot within 20 mm of the start-pose landing point |

## Step 1: servo 2 diagnosis (30 min, driver stopped)

1. Rest the arm on a support, stop `hwr.service`.
2. Read temperature, input voltage and position of every servo (`hwr_core/rrc/board.py`: `servo_temp`,
   `servo_vin`; `tools/live_phase1_bench.py` does this).
3. Read servo 2's angle limits and torque settings if the board exposes them.
4. Restart the stack, then log servo 2 read-back vs. command over a slow up/down sweep with the arm
   extended and folded.

**Decides:** whether servo 2 can carry the arm at reach (fix: load, limits, power) or whether all motion
avoids raising it under load (current workaround).

## Step 2: mount and calibrate the new sensors (60 min)

**Side camera:** low (about 3-5 cm above the sheet) and to the side of the target area, looking across
the target so the tip, its shadow and the dot are all in view during the last 15 mm.

1. Intrinsics: checkerboard, or the grid sheet as for the UGREEN (`tt.ugreen_rig` / `pg.calibrate`).
2. Pose on the sheet: grid crossings visible near the target.
3. Tie to the other cameras with SAM 3.1 on the black dots and the red cube (`model_jab.match_frames`).
4. Check: SAM finds the needle in this view; tip position agrees with the UGREEN to <= 1 mm.

**ToF sensor:** pointing down at the paper. Best on the wrist beside the needle (direct tool height), or
fixed above the target. Calibrate its offset against the triangulated tip height at 5 heights (30, 20,
10, 5, 2 mm). Use: approach slowdown and a hard floor (stop below 2 mm measured), not the final
millimetre.

## Step 3: a first-contact signal (45 min, teleop, slow)

Record 10 slow manual descents onto blank paper with teleop. For each, mark the frame where the tip
visibly touches (side camera: tip meets its shadow or reflection).

Candidates, compared against that ground truth:

| Signal | From | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| tip meets shadow | side camera | earliest, ~0.5 mm |
| ToF stops decreasing | ToF | a few mm, noisy |
| elbow/wrist read-back lag grows | servos | after contact, debounced |
| tip push-back along the needle | arm camera | worked with the straight needle |

Pick the earliest reliable one (likely the side camera) plus a second as backup.

## Step 4: continuous camera streams and a replay harness (60 min, no robot needed)

1. A reader thread per camera (UGREEN, side camera) at 5-10 Hz, frames time-stamped, logged during
   every move.
2. Tip tracking during motion: SAM 3.1 on the side view, motion difference on the UGREEN.
3. **Replay harness:** run `model_jab` (and the new descent) on recorded frame logs with the robot I/O
   stubbed, so every change is checked offline before it moves the arm. Today's logs are the first
   test set.

## Step 5: the watched final approach (60 min)

Replace the jab and the wrist turn with a measured descent:

1. Placing as today (model move, SAM tie, measured corrections) to about 10 mm above and slightly
   behind the dot, without shoulder rises.
2. Descend in 0.5 mm steps along a straight line to the dot. After each step the side camera and the
   UGREEN measure tip vs. dot; sideways and along errors are corrected (pan, wrist roll on the angled
   needle) before the next step.
3. Below 3 mm: 0.2 mm steps, the contact signal watched continuously; stop on the first contact.
4. Log the first-contact frame, the tip position at that frame and the dot position.
