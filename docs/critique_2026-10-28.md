## 1. Verdict

| Doc | Assessment |
|---|---|
| `docs/rca_touch_dot.md` | **Diagnostically sound.** The central finding — the final centimetre is executed open-loop and the success metric was end pose instead of first contact — is correct and well evidenced. Weaknesses: run arithmetic does not add up, several numbers are inside the measurement noise of the tool that produced them, its own top recommendation is dropped downstream, and it never names the design that guarantees dragging. |
| `docs/plan_2026-10-28.md` | **A list of correct intentions, with a critical path that contradicts itself and asks for precision the stack cannot deliver.** In three separate places the gate is tighter than the resolution of the actuators or the measurement chain, and no part of the plan addresses that gap. No fallback if the gate is unmeatable. |

The single biggest structural problem, shared by both docs:

> **The gate (first contact within 1 mm) is smaller than the smallest unit of motion and the smallest
> unit of measurement the rig currently has.**

Repo evidence: `tools/aim_sim.py:113` — "the miss floor is half a pan pulse (~1.5 mm) plus the blind
descent". `tools/model_jab.py:52` — `SIDE_TOL_MM = 1.2 # sideways: half a pan pulse (~2.2 mm/pulse
here); the jab cannot correct sideways`. The plan targets 1 mm.

### 2.3 Analytical gaps

1. **No measurement-uncertainty budget.** The RCA quotes "0.4–1.1 mm miss", "hover to ~1 mm
   repeatedly", "3 mm short" while the instruments that produced those numbers declare looser
   tolerances: `tools/model_jab.py:46` `MATCH_MM = 3.0` (two-camera dot agreement after the fit),
   `:45` `CUBE_TOL_MM = 25.0` ("its centre is rough … 17 mm off"), `docs/handover.md` ("the arm grid's
   y jitters 2-3 mm and slips by whole 5 mm squares"). Every claim under ~2 mm is at or inside noise.
   The RCA should state its own error bars; the human observation of "5 mm short and dragged" is the
   strongest datum in the document and the only one that is model-free.

2. **Cause ranking is asserted, not tested.** §1 says causes are "ranked by how much they explain", but
   nothing separates the effect of an unwatched final centimetre from the effect of 3–25 mm servo
   error. That split is answerable offline from the logs already recorded — which is §9.6's own first
   process fix.

3. **Silent on the design that guarantees drag.** `tools/model_jab.py:104` —
   `--press`, default **0.3 mm into the paper**; `:514` refuses a jab ending more than 0.5 mm past it;
   `:189` records "the jab touched down 5-9 mm off and pressed up to 6.7 mm". A move designed to end
   below the sheet surface cannot produce a clean first contact: any early touchdown drags by
   construction. No fix list includes setting `--press 0` and stopping on contact.

5. **Some of it describes dead code as current behaviour.** RCA §2's table gives `model_jab`'s final
   motion as "one fluid model move, then 8 waypoints". That path is unreachable: `tools/model_jab.py:500`
   `return` ends the function, followed by `:501` "the old jab below is kept for reference but no longer
   reached" (waypoints built at `:518-524`). The live final contact is the **wrist-arc loop** at
   `tools/model_jab.py:478-489`, which *does* re-measure height after every step from the arm-camera grid
   pose (`h = -tip_sheet(rot, t)[2]`, `break` at `h <= 0.3`). So "every contact was made by a motion no
   sensor watched" held for the logged drag runs and no longer holds exactly for the current code. What
   does hold: the watched quantity is a **camera-model height, not a contact signal**, in steps of
   `WRIST_STEP = 3` pulses (`:67`, ≈ 1.3 mm/step) separated by `time.sleep(0.9)` (≈ 1 Hz), measured only in
   the arm view where the dot is hidden (`HIDE_MM`). Claims should be pinned to run IDs or commits, which
   the RCA never does.

6. **`lam` drift is understated.** §3 reports tip depth 202.5 → 206 → **188 mm**. Height comes from the
   tip pixel's ray at depth `lam` (`tools/aim_touch.py:5`, `--lam 206`), so an 18 mm `lam` error offsets
   the whole descent schedule by 18 mm — larger than any single servo error blamed. The RCA treats it
   as a contributing note, not a primary term.

5. **Its own #1 fix is dropped downstream.** §9.1 asks for a camera-independent contact signal
   (conductive needle + foil under the sheet, or high-rate servo load). `docs/plan_2026-10-28.md`
   Step 3's candidate table contains no conductive option. `docs/needle_on_dot.md` Principle 5
   ("every touch leaves a mark; measure mark vs dot") is also dropped. Two camera-free ground-truth
   methods already recommended by two documents are absent from the plan that builds on them.

### 3.2 What the plan does not consider

1. **The cheapest fix is not first.** RCA §2 states the UGREEN already sees tip and dot together, and
   "not reading it during the final motion was my design choice, not a limitation". The plan puts the
   critical path on the riskiest new component instead — new camera, new intrinsics, new three-way tie,
   plus SAM on a needle SAM cannot segment. Continuous closed-loop descent on the **existing** UGREEN
   needs no new calibration and should precede the side camera, which should be the contingency for the
   occlusion case.

2. **The day is all Milestone 1 of 5.** `docs/handover.md` milestones 3–5 (colony detection under Hue,
   pick and transfer, Meta/Llama classify) are what the pitch depends on, and `docs/plan.md` notes the
   Meta model call is not yet made at all. The plan spends 4.5 h on a single retry of milestone 1, with
   no parallel track and no branch for "servo 2 cannot be fixed". For a hackathon, the schedule risk is
   the plan itself.

3. **The run table cannot test the RCA.** §6 columns are attempt / change / first contact / signal /
   pass. The RCA's independent variables are missing: target distance from the natural landing point
   (root cause 3), servo-2 command vs read-back and arm extension (root cause 2), `lam` used, which
   sensor produced each height read (root cause 4), drag distance after touchdown (root cause 1), needle
   angle. Without them a pass proves nothing and a failure cannot be attributed.

4. **No occlusion analysis.** Rule 5 puts the target within 20 mm of the natural foot — the region most
   likely hidden by the arm body in the overhead UGREEN view, and where the arm camera has the dot behind
   the tip (`HIDE_MM`). Rule 5 and Step 2 geometry are never reconciled.

5. **No tool-integrity policy.** After four-plus hard presses and a grasp change that moved `lam` by
   18 mm, there is no step that re-measures `lam` (triangulated, both cameras) before each gate run, and
   no check for a bent needle or a loosened grip between attempts. RCA §9.5 asks for exactly this.

6. **No paper/needle wear accounting.** Repeated descents onto the same sheet degrade the very dots used
   as targets and the ink-mark ground truth.

## 4. Corrections, in priority order

1. **Reconcile the gate with the hardware, or change the actuator.** Either state a defensible gate
   (≤ 2 mm, i.e. half a pan pulse) or specify the mechanism for 1 mm. The only axis in the record that can
   do it is **roll (servo 5): tip 23.6 mm off the roll axis, ~0.1 mm/pulse sideways, "fine adjust without
   moving the camera"** (`docs/plan.md:144`) — and it is mentioned once in passing in plan Step 5.2 ("wrist
   roll on the angled needle") with no calibration step and no Jacobian column. Calibrate roll's mm/pulse at
   the actual descent pose before Step 5. Pan cannot do it (2.95 mm/pulse, `DEADBAND = 2`, `BACKLASH = 5`, a
   reversal jumps ~20 mm per `tools/model_jab.py:362`), and the gateway refused the strafing alternative
   ("drive outside bounded no-reverse envelope", `tools/model_jab.py:365`) — so **both** sideways corrections
   Rule 6 relies on are constrained: pan by backlash, strafe by the no-reverse envelope and the 0.6 s budget.
   Finish every lateral correction from one direction, as the existing discipline already requires.
2. **Add an error-budget table** — per-camera pose error, two-camera tie error (3 mm), `lam` error (18 mm
   observed drift), contact-signal latency, actuator quantisation (1.1–2.95 mm/pulse), and require their
   sum to sit under the gate. One table settles whether the 1 mm gate is physically reachable at all.
3. **Make Step 5 a three-delta change to the live wrist arc, and make it approach along the needle
   axis.** Close all lateral error at ≥ 8 mm, then descend with no lateral command. On the existing arc
   (`tools/model_jab.py:478-489`): step `WRIST_STEP` 3 → 1 pulse, replace the `h <= 0.3` model-height break
   with a contact signal, and take the measurement from a view where the tip and the dot are both visible.
   Set `--press 0.0`, log the drag distance, fix and record the needle angle, and delete or clearly mark the
   unreachable jab block at `tools/model_jab.py:501-535` so the next reader does not analyse dead code —
   the RCA already did.
4. **Reorder the day.** (a) 30 min offline on the existing logs and extend `tools/aim_sim.py` — which
   already models deadband, backlash and blind descent and reports contact rate and miss — to quantify
   how much of the drag is actuator error and how much is sensing error. (b) Continuous-loop descent on
   the existing UGREEN alone. (c) Side camera and ToF only as contingency for occlusion. The replay
   harness moves to the front: it is RCA §9.6's own first fix.
5. **Put a camera-independent ground truth back into Step 3.** Conductive needle + foil under the sheet
   (RCA §9.1) and post-run ink-mark measurement (`docs/needle_on_dot.md` P5). Each is ~15 min and makes
   the 1 mm claim falsifiable instead of camera-asserted.
6. **Restate Rule 4** as "SAM 3.1 for dots and the cube; motion difference for the tip", and state the
   loop-rate budget (SAM ≈ 1–3 Hz). Consider a classical tip tracker in the side view instead of SAM.
7. **Make Step 0 diagnostic of the real fault.** Add a servo-2 rise check **with the arm extended and
   loaded** (the condition that failed), not only at the start pose. Give Step 1 the real paths in
   `D:/robot/highwonder_rework/`.
8. **Add a fallback deliverable.** If Step 1 concludes servo 2 cannot rise under load, say what the day
   ships instead — automatic approach to 5 mm with a teleop finish, or Milestone 3. One sentence removes
   the plan's biggest risk.
9. **Reconcile the four documents.** `docs/plan.md`, `docs/needle_on_dot.md`, `docs/handover.md` and
   `docs/plan_2026-10-28.md` overlap and conflict: `docs/needle_on_dot.md` P1 says "Do not switch to
   model-based IK", while RCA §10 praises the URDF placing move and plan Step 5.1 keeps it. Mark which
   document is current and point `README.md` and `docs/handover.md` at it.
10. **Fix the RCA's numbers and commit its evidence.** Recompute the run table so buckets account for
    every run (107, not 95), resolve 8 vs 12 and 2 vs 4, add `arm_touch`/teleop or state why they are
    excluded, pin every claim to a run ID or commit, and commit the frames behind the load-bearing claims
    (`contact_ugreen_zoom.png` for the 22:46 and 22:51 runs) under `evidence/`.

## 5. Addendum — four further findings, verified in code

`docs/critique_rca_plan_2026-10-28.md` (11 KB, written 00:45, a parallel review of the same two docs) reaches
the same headline conclusion. Its distinct findings, checked here and accepted:

| Finding | Verification |
|---|---|
| **Step 1 invalidates Step 0.** Step 1.4 restarts the stack, and `docs/handover.md` records that `sensor_supervisor_node` "comes back on every stack restart" — so Step 0's first gate (no supervisor, arm frame < 0.3 s) is void by the time Step 5 runs. The plan never re-kills it. | ✓ `docs/handover.md:51-53`, `docs/plan.md:160` |
| **Step 4 is not 60 minutes.** `tools/model_jab.py` is 563 lines with `main()` at `:99` and ten nested closures over live I/O (`note:115`, `sam_needle_tip:129`, `triangulate:141`, `view:170`, `wrist_land:186`, `floor_ok:198`, `sam_find:204`, `cube_centre:215`, `retie:290`, `arm_fwd:324`). Stubbing robot I/O for replay is a refactor of that structure, not an addition — and "today's logs are the first test set" points at logs that are not in the repo. | ✓ measured line counts and def list |
| **SAM is file-in / session-per-call, so loop cost is worse than the inference time alone.** `tools/sam_detect.py:35-41`: `start_session` with `resource_path = <image path>`, one prompt, `close_session` in `finally`; `model_jab` writes a PNG per detection (`:132-133`, `:207-208`). No latency is measured or budgeted anywhere in either document. Design the loop as fast classical tracking during motion with SAM as a periodic re-anchor — which is what the working code already does (`moved_needle` + `SAM_AREA`/`SAM_BLACK_FRAC` filters at `tools/model_jab.py:41-43`, i.e. SAM is *already* gated by hand-made thresholds, so Rule 4 misdescribes the present system). | ✓ |
| **Rule 5 is a diagnostic scaffold, not a fix — and risks a false pass.** Moving the notebook so the target sits within 20 mm of the natural foot tests the arm, but colonies on a plate cannot be moved to the arm. If the plan does not label it as scaffolding, a passed gate gets over-read — the same metric error RCA §6.3 confesses to. | ✓ by construction; needs one sentence in the plan. |

Also accepted: the Step 3 ground truth is circular as written — the "frame where the tip visibly touches"
(tip meets its shadow, side camera) is scored by the same camera that will control the descent, and 10 slow
**manual teleop** descents differ in speed and compliance from the automatic descent they are meant to
calibrate. An independent contact signal breaks both problems at once.

## 7. What is worth keeping

From RCA §10, all of it, and it is the strongest part of the two documents — two-camera SAM calibration
on the dots and cube, URDF for the large placing move, tip depth triangulated to 0.9 mm, repeatable ~1 mm
hover, commanded-pose tracking (never re-based on read-back), e-stop removed, gateway limits understood.
`tools/aim_sim.py` is an underused asset: it already encodes deadband, backlash, joint error and blind
descent, and asserts an achievable-miss floor — it is the natural home for the experiments the plan needs
before any new hardware is mounted.
